This directory contains 32 of the 8,000 one-second 16 kHz mono WAV clips
from the mini_speech_commands archive (4 clips per command label). See
https://www.tensorflow.org/datasets/catalog/speech_commands for the full
dataset, labeling, and citation requirements.
